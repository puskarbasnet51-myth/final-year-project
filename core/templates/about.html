About FeedForward

A platform built on the belief that anyone can make a difference — one fresh meal at a time.

What is FeedForward?

FeedForward is a web-based Fresh Individual Food Donation Management Platform that connects individuals who wish to donate freshly prepared meals with NGOs, orphanages, and old age homes in need of regular food support.

Unlike other platforms that deal with leftover or surplus food, FeedForward focuses exclusively on fresh, intentionally prepared meals — either home-cooked or ordered from a local restaurant — ensuring every recipient receives food with dignity and care.

Our Mission

To make food donation simple, dignified, and accessible for every individual in Nepal — turning personal celebrations, religious occasions, and everyday goodwill into organized, trackable social impact.

What Makes FeedForward Different

Fresh Food Only

Every meal donated through FeedForward is freshly prepared — home-cooked or restaurant-ordered. No leftovers. No surplus. Just fresh food made with care.

Smart Matching

Our AI-based Smart Matching system automatically connects donors with the most suitable receiving organization based on location, meal needs, and date.

Dignity in Giving

Recipients receive freshly prepared meals — not leftovers. This reflects a genuine culture of care, respect, and intentional giving.
